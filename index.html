League Phase Simulator

36 teams in 4 pots of 9. Every team plays 8 matches: two opponents from each pot, one at home and one away. Win 3, draw 1, loss 0. The default settings are fitted to betting odds. Move a slider and the simulation re-runs in your browser.

Each pot is split into a stronger group (a) and a weaker group (b). Percentages are the share of team-seasons ending in that zone.

Rows are the home group, columns the away group. These update live as you move the sliders and are averaged over the random team strengths.

Champions League bookmaker odds for 57 league-phase games of 2026-27 (matchdays 1-4), bookmaker margin removed, averaged by pot pairing. The default settings were fitted to these; the Europa League preset sits halfway between the Champions League fit and a separate fit to 13 Europa League games (matchdays 3-4).

Ties on points are broken by wins, then at random, since goals are not modelled. Chips show matchdays 1-8: W win, T draw, L loss. Hover a chip for the opponent.

How it works: each simulated season draws a fresh fixture list, nudges the settings by a small random amount, gives every team its own random strength, then plays all 144 matches. A match's odds come from the strength gap plus home advantage (the log-odds of winning rather than losing); the chance of a draw falls as the gap grows. Defaults were fitted to bookmaker odds for 57 league-phase games of 2026-27.
